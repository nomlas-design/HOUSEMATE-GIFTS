# Project collaboration instructions

This repository is dedicated to the balcony planter riser and enclosed litter-tray cabinet project.

Before changing the design:

1. Read `PROJECT.md`, `model-spec.json`, and the latest entry in `handoff/HANDOVER.md`.
2. Treat owner measurements as authoritative. Dimensions supplied from the product listing are retailer/listing-derived, regardless of which contributor recorded them.
3. Keep `models/planter-stand-model.html`, `model-spec.json`, and `deliverables/bom/planter-riser-bom.xlsx` consistent whenever component geometry or IDs change.
4. Put new source photographs and sketches under `references/`. Put housemate-specific additions under `references/housemate/` when provenance would otherwise be unclear.
5. Append a concise entry to `handoff/HANDOVER.md` before handing work to another person or model.
6. Do not treat this exploratory design as structural certification. Keep unknown loads, fasteners, balcony capacity, and unconfirmed products explicit.
